Privacy Policy

Who is behind this?

This site and the research are run by me — Vukory, a master's student at University of Zenica 🇧🇦.

Analytics

This site uses Plausible for analytics. Plausible is a privacy-friendly, Open Source tool that uses no cookies. It's self-hosted by an SVGO maintainer, and the dashboard is fully public, so you can see exactly what I see. No secrets :3

Plausible only picks up general information like which pages were visited, how long people stayed, and rough device info (device type, OS, country, browser). Nothing that points back to you. Also, if you use an ad blocker like uBlock Origin, it'll block Plausible by default.

Taking the survey?

When you submit your response, your answers travel securely (HTTPS) to a server I control. Along with your answers, the server also logs your IP address and browser details. I use this information to catch duplicate or spam submissions, and they get deleted together with any emails once the research wraps up.

Your answers only ever appear in an aggregated form, fully anonymized in my thesis, never tied back to you.

Your email

Leaving your email is completely optional! It does exactly one job: allowing me to send you your single-use code for a free sticker pack. Once that code's been sent, your email has done its job and I will delete it, as there's no reason to hang onto it.

How long do I keep data?

  • Emails and IP addresses - deleted when the research ends (expected Q4 2027), or upon request
  • Your answers - kept anonymized as part of the thesis

Any questions? Feel free to reach out to me at hi@vukory.art! 💌
